    How to read this map

    The whole planet, measured from one spot

    This is a Mercator map with its pole moved to a place you choose. Mercator enlarges land without limit as it nears the pole, so your chosen spot fills the bottom of the screen at street level, and everything else shrinks with distance until the far side of the Earth is a thin band at the top.

    Each column is one compass direction from the pole, labelled along the top. Height is distance, read off the ruler on the right; every step up the same number of pixels multiplies the distance by the same factor. Shapes stay true everywhere. Only size changes.

    Drag / scroll
    Up and down travels away from or toward the pole. Sideways turns you around it.
    Pinch / Ctrl + scroll
    Widen or narrow the map.
    Double-click
    Fly there and make it the new pole. A single click shows the distance and direction.
    Keys
    Arrows move. Plus and minus widen or narrow. N turns north up. The slash key opens search.
